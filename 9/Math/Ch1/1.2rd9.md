# Natural Numbers
N = {1,2,3,4,5,6.....inf} 
are also known as counting numbers, begin with 1 to infinity. Collection of *natural numbers* is denoted by $N$
$$
	N = \{ 1,2,3,4,\dots \infty  \}
$$

Include 0 with all the Natural numbers, this collection of numbers are called *Whole numbers* denoted with $W$
$W$ = {0,1,2,3,4,5,6...inf}
$$
	W = \{ 0,1,2,3,4\dots \infty \}
$$

Denote 0 as origin, (O). 
If we mirror whole numbers over origin. we get new numbers, image of 1,2,3,4 now denoted as -1,-2,-3,-4. combination of negative and positive whole numbers with 0 as origin is are called *Integers*
we denote Integers as $Z$, positive integers are $Z^+$ & negative integers are $Z^-$
$$
	Z = \{-\infty..,-3,-2,-1,0, 1,2,3,\dots.\infty\}
$$
Z = {-inf....-3,-2,-1,0,1,2,3,.....inf}


*Rational Numbesr* are numbers written in form of p/q form , where q != 0 & p,q belong to integers. These are decimal numbers.

$$
\text{Q} = \left\{  \frac{p}{q}  | q\neq 0 \& p,q \in Z \right\}
$$
### Example 2
Find a rational number between -2 & 6
Formula $x<\frac{x+y}{2}<y$
therefore, a rational number between -2 and 6 is 4/2 or 2.
